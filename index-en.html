What Is Sim-Narrative?

Sim-narrative is an original literary method developed by Andrey Narrativ. It uses a layered approach to fiction, allowing different ways of presenting events to complement one another.

  • The literary layer — action, imagery, and the emotional development of the story.
  • The reflective layer — a character's inner experience, including thoughts, diary entries, memories, and personal assessments.
  • The documentary layer — protocols, reports, records, testimony, and other forms that show events from another perspective.
  • Additional layers — authorial metacommentary and descriptions of the narrative process, where the work calls for them.

The layers can be combined differently from one work to another. Their purpose is not to make a text complicated for its own sake, but to reveal events, characters, and connections that a single narrative mode might not fully convey.

About the Author

Andrey Narrativ is a Russian writer and the creator of the sim-narrative literary method. His work spans psychological fiction, thrillers, horror, mystery, satire, and science fiction.

His work appears in literary publications, including the anthology Russian Literature of the 21st Century, the journal Dvurechye, and the literary almanac Tsaritsyn. He also produces audio editions of his own work and serves as a member of the judging panel for the 9th International Science Fiction Competition, the Bradbury Cup.
