theory, philosophy and practice of sim‑narrative

Sim‑narrative is both a literary method and a new genre. As a method, it sets engineering rules: three obligatory layers (event, reflection, system), their conflict, rejection of authorial authoritarianism. As a genre, it offers readers a volumetric narrative where truth is born at the intersection of contradictory versions.

This section collects manifestos, philosophical essays and articles explaining the method, its cultural roots, and its application in literature, AI training, and everyday writing.
